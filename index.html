🧁 Chef's notes
How to Play · Cheems' Candy Factory
1
🍭 Insertion Line
One candy drops through the chute at a time. Toss it onto the glowing + gumdrop pad where its number fits, so the tray stays sorted smallest to biggest.
Keys: ← → to pick a pad, Enter to place.
2
🥣 Merge Mixer
Two trays slide in already sorted. Compare the candy at the front of each tray and push the smaller one into the big mixer.
Keys: A or ← for Tray A, B or → for Tray B.
3
⭐ Quick Splitter
The machine crowns a random golden star candy. Send smaller candies to the left bin and bigger ones to the right bin until the pile is empty.
Keys: 1 for the smaller bin, 2 for the bigger bin.
4
🎓 Tutorial & sweet streaks
Tutorial sorts candies numbered 1 to 11 with fixed green shades and highlight rings. Use ↩ Undo (or Z / Backspace) anytime to rewind steps. In Hands-on Mode, keep placing candies correctly to grow your sweet streak - long streaks fire confetti cannons and shout SWEET!, TASTY! and DELICIOUS!
5
🍯 Don't jam the factory
A wrong drop bonks Cheems with his club and removes a segment from the Factory HP bar. Empty the HP bar and the whole factory jams - press R or click FIX THE MACHINE to sweep up and try again.
Other keys: Space play/pause in Tutorial, Z / Backspace undo, M mute, R restart.
